# G5 Auto — Used-Car Business Operating System

Premium used-car business operating system focused on Charlotte, NC.

## Highlights
- Centralized investment / profit / ROI / margin calculations.
- Charlotte market snapshot calibrated from current marketplace data.
- Model-year market anchors plus observed local retail examples.
- Deal Analyzer with buy grade, max purchase, projected resale, ROI, margin, and comp links.
- Inventory cards open the full vehicle workspace.
- Premium Full Details modal with hero image, KPI strip, and spec panels.
- Automatic Wikimedia Commons / Wikipedia vehicle-image lookup with generated fallback.
- Dashboard realized revenue + projected exit outlook.
- Analytics revenue/investment bars, profit trend, and expense mix charts.
- Inventory aging / capital-at-risk signals.
- Configurable search sites in `config.js` (CarGurus, Edmunds, AutoTrader, TrueCar, Carvana).
- Optional demo mode: append `?demo=1` to the URL on a fresh browser profile.

## Data note
Market figures are point-in-time snapshots, not guarantees or live appraisals. The app exposes marketplace links so the operator can verify current comps before buying.
